## **Features**
- Quickly see all your active subscriptions for each month using FSCalendar..
- Choose from a large selection of companies when adding subscriptions.
- A seamless form powered by Eureka simplifies the process of adding new subscriptions.
- Track your expenses by category, monthly costs, and total subscription spending.
- Securely store and retrieve subscription data and companies.
- Stay on top of your upcoming subscription renewals with timely reminders.

## **Technologies Used**
- UIKit - Core UI framework for iOS
- FSCalendar - Interactive calendar UI
- Eureka - Dynamic form builder
- Firebase - Backend for storing subscription data
- Push Notifications - Timely reminders for upcoming renewals


## **Permissions**

To use this package, your app must have **Accessibility permissions**, and **App Sandbox must be disabled**. **Accessibility permissions** can be configured in **System Preferences > Privacy & Security > Accessibility**. Ensure that your app is checked in the list of allowed apps.
